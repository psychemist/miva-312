Is this a confirmed appointment?

It’s a request for your preferred time. Our team will contact you by phone or email to confirm availability before your visit.

Do I need an account to book?

No. You’re welcome to request an appointment without signing up. Creating an account is optional.

What should I bring to our first visit?

Bring any vaccination records, a list of current medications, and your questions. A lead or carrier helps keep everyone comfortable.

Can I shop for pet essentials?

Yes, you can browse pet essentials when you visit the store. Online appointments cover consultations, vaccinations, wellness checks, and grooming.
